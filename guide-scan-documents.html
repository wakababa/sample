How to Scan Documents to PDF on Android

Your phone camera is a capable document scanner when paired with the right app. Instead of blurry photos, you can produce crisp, cropped, properly-oriented PDF scans of contracts, receipts, ID cards, and handwritten notes — ready to email or archive.

What you'll need

Just your Android phone with PDF Manager installed and camera permission granted. The scanner works offline, so you can capture documents anywhere — no signal required.

Step-by-step: scanning a document

  1. Open the Scanner. Launch PDF Manager and tap the Document Scanner tool.
  2. Position the page. Place your document on a flat, contrasting surface and hold the phone steady above it. Good, even lighting makes a big difference.
  3. Let auto edge-detection work. The app finds the document borders and applies perspective correction, so a photo taken at a slight angle still comes out as a straight rectangle.
  4. Choose a filter. Pick black & white for text-heavy pages (smaller, crisper), grayscale for a neutral look, or color to preserve stamps and highlights.
  5. Add more pages if needed. Capture additional pages to build a multi-page PDF in one file.
  6. Save as PDF. Export the scan as a PDF stored on your device.
Tip: For the sharpest results, avoid shadows from your own hand or phone. Rest your elbows on the table to keep the camera stable, and tap the page to focus before capturing.

Make scans searchable with OCR

A plain scan is just an image of text — you can't search or copy from it. With the OCR feature (available in the Pro tier), PDF Manager recognizes the text in your scan and produces a searchable PDF, all processed on-device for privacy. That means you can later find a scanned invoice by searching for a word inside it.

Scanning tips for common documents

  • Receipts: use black & white and crop tight to keep file size small.
  • ID cards: capture on a dark background so edge detection locks onto the card.
  • Contracts: scan every page in order, then confirm nothing is cut off in the preview.
  • Handwritten notes: grayscale usually keeps pencil and pen legible.

Frequently asked questions

Do my scans get uploaded anywhere?
No. Scanning and processing happen on your device. Your documents are not sent to a server.
Can I combine a scan with an existing PDF?
Yes — save the scan, then use the Merge tool to join it with other PDFs.
Why is my scan blurry?
Usually motion or focus. Hold steady, tap to focus, and make sure there's enough light before capturing.
