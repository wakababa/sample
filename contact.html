Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you. Whether you've found a bug, have a feature idea, or need help using a tool, get in touch and we'll do our best to respond.

Email support

The fastest way to reach the PDF Manager team:

pdfmanagerapp@gmail.com

We typically reply within 2–3 business days.

What to include

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • Your device model and Android version
  • The app version (found in the app's settings screen)
  • A clear description of what happened and what you expected
  • Screenshots, if the issue is visual

Feature requests

PDF Manager is actively developed, and much of what's in the app came from user suggestions. If there's a tool or improvement you'd like to see, send it our way — we read every message.

Privacy & data

For questions about how your data is handled, please read our Privacy Policy. In short: core PDF processing happens offline on your device, and we don't collect your documents.
