About PDF Manager

PDF Manager is an all-in-one, offline-first PDF toolkit for Android. It lets you merge, split, scan, compress, protect, convert, and edit PDF documents directly on your device — no account, no cloud uploads, and no complicated setup.

Our mission

Most people don't need a heavy desktop program or a paid web service just to combine two PDFs or scan a receipt. We built PDF Manager to make everyday document tasks fast, private, and genuinely free for the core tools. Whether you're a student bundling lecture notes, a professional preparing a contract, or someone who just needs to turn a photo into a PDF, the app is designed to get out of your way and finish the job in seconds.

What makes it different

Offline processing

Core tools run entirely on your phone. Your files are never uploaded to a server to be merged, split, or compressed.

Privacy-first

No forced sign-up and no account required. We don't collect your documents or personal data.

Free where it matters

Merge, split, scan, compress, protect, rotate, watermark, and image-to-PDF are free to use.

AI when you need it

An optional Pro tier adds an on-device AI PDF Assistant and OCR for searchable PDFs.

What you can do

  • Merge & split — combine multiple PDFs or extract specific pages and ranges.
  • Scan documents — use your camera with automatic edge detection and perspective correction.
  • Image to PDF — turn photos into a clean, shareable PDF.
  • Compress — shrink file size by up to 80% for email and messaging.
  • Protect — add 128-bit password encryption to sensitive files.
  • Rotate & watermark — fix page orientation and add text watermarks.
  • Convert (Pro) — PDF to Word (DOCX) and PDF to images (PNG/JPG).
  • OCR & AI (Pro) — make scans searchable and ask questions about your documents on-device.

Built for everyone

PDF Manager is available in six languages — English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, and Arabic — with a modern interface and a comfortable dark mode. It's used by students, freelancers, office workers, and anyone who needs quick PDF operations on mobile.
