How to Merge PDF Files on Android

Combining several PDFs into a single document is one of the most common tasks people need on their phone — bundling receipts for an expense report, joining scanned contract pages, or gathering study notes into one file. Here's how to do it in a few taps, completely offline.

Why merge PDFs?

A single PDF is easier to send, print, and archive than a dozen loose files. Instead of attaching ten separate pages to an email, you attach one tidy document in the exact order you want. Because PDF Manager processes files on your device, your documents never leave your phone — which matters when you're merging contracts, bank statements, or medical records.

Step-by-step: merging PDFs

  1. Open PDF Manager and tap the Merge PDFs tool on the home screen.
  2. Select the files. Choose two or more PDFs from your device storage. You can add files from different folders in the same session.
  3. Reorder the pages. Drag and drop the documents into the order you want them to appear in the final file. This is where you fix "page 3 should come before page 2."
  4. Preview before saving. Check the combined result so there are no surprises — right pages, right order.
  5. Merge and save. Tap merge, and the app writes a single new PDF to your device. Your original files stay untouched.
Tip: Give the merged file a clear name (for example, Expenses-March-2026.pdf) so it's easy to find later. If you only need certain pages from a large PDF, use the Split tool first to extract them, then merge.

Common use cases

  • Students: combine lecture slides, handouts, and your own notes into one revision file.
  • Freelancers: merge an invoice, a timesheet, and a receipt into a single client-ready document.
  • Everyone: join scanned pages of a signed form that came out as separate images or PDFs.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit to how many PDFs I can merge?
You can combine multiple files at once. Very large documents simply take a little longer to process because everything happens on your device.
Will merging reduce quality?
No. Merging preserves the original pages as-is. If you want a smaller file afterward, run it through the Compress tool.
Does merging work offline?
Yes. The Merge tool runs entirely on your phone — no internet connection and no account required.
Is it free?
Yes, merging PDFs is part of the free tier in PDF Manager.
