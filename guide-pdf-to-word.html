How to Convert a PDF to Word (DOCX)

PDFs are great for sharing a finished document, but not for editing one. When you need to change the wording of a contract, update a résumé, or reuse text from a report, converting the PDF to an editable Word (DOCX) file saves you from retyping everything.

Before you start

PDF to Word conversion is part of the Pro converter workflow in PDF Manager. It works best on PDFs that contain real, selectable text. If your PDF is a scanned image of text, run OCR first so the characters are recognized — otherwise the converter has only a picture to work with.

Step-by-step: PDF to Word

  1. Open the Converter in PDF Manager and choose PDF to Word (DOCX).
  2. Select your PDF. Pick the document you want to make editable.
  3. Convert. The app processes the file and produces a DOCX you can open in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or any compatible editor.
  4. Open and edit. Review the result and adjust formatting where needed — then save or export as you like.
Tip: Conversion accuracy depends heavily on the original layout. Simple, text-first documents convert almost perfectly. Complex multi-column designs, tables, and heavy graphics may need light formatting cleanup afterward — that's true of every PDF-to-Word tool, not just this one.

Getting the best results

  • Start from a text PDF, not a photo of a page. Selectable text converts cleanly.
  • For scans, apply OCR first to turn the image into recognized text.
  • Expect to tidy tables. Complex tables are the hardest element for any converter; budget a minute to realign them.
  • Keep the original PDF as your reference while you edit the Word copy.

Other conversions

Besides Word, the converter can also turn a PDF into images (PNG/JPG) — handy when you need a single page as a picture for a slide or a chat message. And if you're going the other way, the Image to PDF tool bundles photos back into a PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my converted document look slightly different?
PDF and Word handle layout differently. Fonts, spacing, and tables may shift a little; a quick cleanup usually fixes it.
Can I convert a scanned PDF?
Yes, but apply OCR first so the text is recognized. Converting a pure image without OCR gives you a picture, not editable text.
Is my document uploaded to convert?
PDF Manager is built offline-first and keeps your files on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
